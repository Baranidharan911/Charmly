No. 05 · At the counter

Things people usually ask.

Q.01

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no ads, no trial. Download it and it's yours.

Q.02

Will it block my clicks?

No. Clicks pass through to the windows underneath. Only the charms respond, and only when you grab them.

Q.03

Will it slow my computer down?

Barely. It only draws while a charm is moving. Once they settle, it goes quiet until you touch one again.

Q.04

Can I use my own picture?

Yes. In the charm panel choose “Import an image”. Pictures with a transparent background look best.

Q.05

How do I hide the charms or quit?

Right-click the Charm Line icon in your system tray. From there you can hide them, turn on start at login, or quit.

Q.06

Does it collect any data?

No. It works fully offline. Your charms, settings and pictures stay on your computer.

Q.07

Can it start with Windows?

Yes. Right-click the tray icon and tick “Start at login”.

Q.08

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Charm Line → Uninstall. On a Mac, drag it from Applications to the Bin.
