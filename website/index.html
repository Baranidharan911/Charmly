Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There's no account and no sign-up, and it works offline.

Will it get in the way of my work?

No. Clicks go straight through to the windows underneath. Only the charms themselves respond to your mouse.

How do I hide the charms or quit?

Right-click the Charm Line icon in the system tray. From there you can hide the charms, turn on start at login, or quit.

How do I uninstall it?

Go to Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Charm Line, and choose Uninstall.
