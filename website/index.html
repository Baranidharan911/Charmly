Everything worth asking

Good questions,
honest answers.

Is Charm Line really free?

Yes. There's no account, no ads and no trial. Download it and it's yours.

Will it get in the way while I work?

No. Clicks go straight through to the windows underneath. Only the charms respond to your mouse, and only when you grab them.

Does it slow down my computer?

Barely. Charm Line only draws while something is moving. Once your charms come to rest, it goes quiet until you touch one again.

Can I make a charm from my own picture?

Yes. Open the charm panel, choose “Import an image” and pick a PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG. Images with transparent backgrounds look best.

How do I open the controls, hide the charms, or quit?

Click the Charm Line icon in your system tray, or press Ctrl+Shift+H (⌘⇧H on Mac). Right-click the tray icon to hide the charms, turn on start at login, or quit.

Can it start when my computer starts?

Yes. Right-click the tray icon and tick “Start at login”.

Why does Windows show a warning when I install it?

Windows SmartScreen shows this for new apps that haven't built up a download history yet. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Does Charm Line collect my data?

No. It works fully offline. Your charms, settings and imported images stay on your computer.

How do I uninstall it?

On Windows, open Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Charm Line and choose Uninstall. On Mac, drag it from Applications to the Bin.
